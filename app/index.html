    Prices

    Inflation: 3.1%

    Consumer prices rose 3.1% in the year to October 2025, down from 3.7% in September and inside the office's 2 to 4% target band for the first time since 2021.

    Inflation, 2019 to 2025

    Annual CPI change, %. Source: Consumer Prices, October 2025.

    The 2022 peak of 5.6% followed the energy price rise. Food has driven the index since.

    What rose fastest

    Annual change by CPI group, October 2025. Source: Consumer Prices, October 2025.

    Food rose 4.0%, the fastest group. Transport rose slowest, at 1.8%, as fuel prices eased.

    How the CPI is built

    The index tracks the prices of 412 goods and services in 38 towns, weighted by what households actually buy. Food carries a weight of 28.4%, housing 21.7%, and transport 12.9%. Weights come from the Household Expenditure Survey 2023 and are reviewed every five years. The next weight review lands in 2028.

      Methods in brief

      GDP
      Measured at current prices and at 2020 constant prices. Real growth is the change in the constant-price series. The 2024 census set the population base for per-capita figures.
      Unemployment
      A person counts as unemployed when they did no paid work in the reference week, were available to start within two weeks, and looked for work in the previous four weeks. The survey covers 12,400 households each quarter.
      Inflation
      The consumer price index tracks 412 items in 38 towns. Annual change compares the index with the same month a year earlier.
      Regions
      Regional GDP is compiled from business surveys and tax records, allocated to where production happens. Small-region figures carry wider margins of error.

      Corrections

      No corrections have been issued for the figures on this site. When a figure changes after publication, the correction is listed here with the old and new value and the reason.
